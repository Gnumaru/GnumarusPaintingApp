Welcome to the Gnumaru's Painting and Animation App Manual
================================================================

*Gnumaru's Painting and Animation App* is a frontend-only single page web
application for digital painting, pixel art, vector-style art and
frame-by-frame animation, accelerated with WebGL2. It runs entirely in
your browser: projects live in folders on your disk (Chromium File System
Access API) or in browser storage, and every edit stays on your machine.

.. note::

   This manual is also available `in Brazilian Portuguese <../pt_BR/>`_.
