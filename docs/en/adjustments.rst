Adjustments
===========

Adjust colors
-------------

**Edit > Adjust colors** (also the layers-panel button) opens the
brightness/contrast dialog: two sliders with exact numeric fields,
live preview on release, and ``OK`` (one undo entry) / ``Apply``
(re-base and keep editing) / ``Cancel`` (revert). With a floating
selection, only its masked content is adjusted.

Hue and saturation
------------------

**Edit > Adjust hue/saturation** works the same way for hue,
saturation, lightness and alpha, with HSL/OKHSL model and
additive/proportional mode dropdowns.

Invert
------

**Adjust > Invert colors** needs no dialog: RGB flips, alpha stays.
